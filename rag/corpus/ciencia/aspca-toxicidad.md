# Peace Lily — ASPCA Toxic and Non-toxic Plants

## Toxicity for dogs and cats

Additional Common Names: Mauna Loa Peace Lily. Scientific Name: Spathiphyllum. Family: Araceae.

Toxicity: Toxic to Dogs, Toxic to Cats.

Toxic Principles: Insoluble calcium oxalates.

Clinical Signs: Oral irritation, intense burning and irritation of mouth, tongue and lips, excessive drooling, vomiting, difficulty swallowing.

If you suspect your pet may have ingested a potentially toxic substance, call the ASPCA Poison Control at (888) 426-4435 or contact your local veterinarian as soon as possible.
